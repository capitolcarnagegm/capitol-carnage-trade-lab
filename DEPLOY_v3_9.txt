GM'S LOCKER v3.9 — TRANSACTION MANAGER

NEW:
- Dedicated Transactions page
- Manual transaction moves an asset OFF current roster and ONTO destination roster
- Supports trade, waiver claim, FA signing, cut, roster correction, pick transfer
- Optional salary and contract-year update
- Manual trade can move one asset each direction in one action
- Transaction log records every manual move
- Player/pick ownership source becomes Manual Transaction
- My Roster and War Room have quick action buttons to open Transaction Manager

This solves stale MFL ownership such as a player remaining on the prior franchise after a trade.
